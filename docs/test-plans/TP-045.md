## Driver

With all of TP-040 to TP-044 applied, a three-turn session on a snapshot of the real store (the same questions as the smoke test) and a digest marked numbers that are correct. Re-running the post-check on the stored answers and ledgers gives these causes:

| Class | Live example | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| `hyphen_count` | "the 259-session history", "a 20-session window of -13.769%" | a count noun joined by a hyphen is not a count, so the clause's metric cue claims it |
| `range_noun` | "the 73.3–74.9 session range" | "session" in "session range" is read as a count noun |
| `price_cue_percent` | "the adjusted close fell -3.9234%", "to 73.9 on 2026-10-02 (change -7.044%" | a percent next to a price cue is typed as a price level |
| `nearest_window` | "the 60-session figure (-10.5868%) is a smaller decline than the 20-session figure" | the window rule takes the count after the claim, even when a count before it is nearer |
| `comparison_sign` | "Tencent fell 3.92% versus 3.19% for the index" | the compared number inherits the field but not the decline |
| `index_alias` | "against an essentially flat index (-0.24%)" | only "the index" names the index |
| `section_ref` | "the price moves in Section 2" | "Section N" is not a layout reference |

Correctly flagged, so not changed: the model called a 250-session change a "drawdown" (-34.29%, -9.60%), and that claim is wrong.

## Scope

`agent/guardrail_claims.py` only, claim side:

- **hyphen_count**: a count noun may follow its number after a hyphen.
- **range_noun**: "session" followed by range, high, low or bar is not a count noun.
- **price_cue_percent**: a percent whose resolved field is a price class (price, open, high, low, close, adjusted close) is a `price_change` when the ledger holds one. A price level is never a percent.
- **nearest_window**: of the counts allowed by TP-042's rule, the one nearest the claim selects the window. A count before the claim is allowed when no other percent precedes the claim in its scope, or when no change word lies between the count and the claim.
- **comparison_sign**: a `price_change` claim right after a comparison word (versus, against, than, ...) takes the sign of the last change word earlier in its sentence.
- **index_alias**: "the/an/its" with up to two words before "index" or "benchmark" names the ledger's single index.
- **section_ref**: "Section N" and "Sections N" are layout.

## Method, and its deviation (disclosed)

The rules come from the live answers of the verification run. They are developed on unit cases built from those answers' shapes, on the frozen E-015 ledgers. E-013, E-014 and E-015 have all been looked at and are development sets now, so they run as regression only: the change must add no escape on any of them, and every removed false flag is listed. **No independent battery measures this change.** Whether to commission one (E-016, written by a separately briefed author) before relying on these rules is an owner decision, recorded in the PR.

| Test | Verifies | Kind |
|---|---|---|

| `test_nearest_count_selects_the_window` | "the 60-session figure (-6.58%) is smaller than the 20-session figure (-4.88%)" verifies; swapped values are flagged | positive + negative |

| `test_section_number_is_layout` | "see Section 2" raises no claim; "2 sections" is unaffected | positive + negative |

Mutation checks: one per rule.
