## Mechanisms

1. **Complement break (mis-scoped hedge).** A reporting or cognition verb followed by "that", "why" or "whether" opens a new clause for hedge scope. A hedge in the matrix clause no longer excuses the complement's claim. A hedge inside the complement still excuses it, and "it may be that X" is unchanged, because "be" is not a reporting verb.
2. **Certainty constructions need a price or performance predicate.** The predicate is one of: a movement verb (the existing list), a currency-marked level ("HK$600", "600 HKD", "6,000 points"), or a percentage move with a price noun ("gain 40 percent", "20 percent higher"). Three families:
   - (a) "bound", "destined", "set", "on course", "poised", "primed", "headed", "certain", "sure", "guaranteed" followed by "to" or "for", then a predicate;
   - (b) certainty idioms ("foregone conclusion", "no doubt", "beyond doubt", "matter of timing", "bank on", "no two ways about it", "can't miss", "done deal") with a predicate in the same clause;
   - (c) "will" followed by a price level or a percentage move, with a price noun in the same sentence.
   A scheduled event with no predicate ("set to report results") is not a claim and passes.
3. **Fabricated attribution.** No change to the exemptions. Speech exemptions remain institutional only (ADR-006 Am8), so generic tags ("the consensus view", "our proprietary signal") never excuse. Misses in this class are predicate-coverage misses, addressed by mechanism 2.

Out of scope, kept as a known residual and reported with the result: causal claims without a certainty construction (for example "the price is driven entirely by one memo").

Final code, measured as TP-030's and TP-032's protocols require. Battery 1 is development data, reported for transparency. Battery 2 was measured once, counts only, and is now consumed.

Target (owner, 2026-10-04): recall at least 80% on the flag set and zero false flags on the controls. **Not met**: recall is 47%, and the controls stay clean.

Reading of the result. The development gain outran the sealed gain: mis-scoped hedges went from 8/10 to 3/10, and unanticipated paraphrases from 9/10 to 3/10. The reporting-verb list and the certainty idioms were extended after reading battery 1's misses, so the development numbers overstate generalization. The sealed numbers are the honest estimate.

Residuals, not covered by the approved mechanisms: causal claims with no certainty construction ("the price is driven entirely by one memo"), and present-tense level-by-date claims ("hits 300 HKD by midsummer"). Battery 2 cannot be used again to choose a pattern. Any further claim needs a new sealed battery under a new TP, from a separate author.

Decision for the owner: accept the partial result and revise the target by a new TP, hold the change and author a third sealed battery under a new TP, or stop DE-09 at this point.
