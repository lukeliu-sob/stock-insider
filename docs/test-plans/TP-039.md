## Driver

E-013 (11 of its 20 false flags) found two claim-side reading errors:

- **a. Growth phrasing.** "Apple's revenue grew 9.56% year on year" is typed as revenue, not revenue growth: the revenue cue stands before the number, and "year on year" is not in the growth cue (only "year over year", "YoY", "y/y", "revenue growth"). Five correct claims were flagged.
- **b. A company name that is also a field word.** In a table, the row label "Target" (Target Corp) matches the `target` label field, so every number in the row is read as a price target, which never has evidence. Six correct claims were flagged.

Options c (a scale in a table title) and d (a subject written after its number) are deferred by owner decision.

## Scope

`agent/guardrail_claims.py` only (safety-critical prefix).

- **a.** The growth cue gains "year on year" and its hyphenated form. A percent-valued number whose nearest field cue is `revenue`, joined by a change verb (grew, rose, increased, fell, declined, dropped, was up, was down, and similar), is typed `revenue_growth`. A revenue level ("revenue of USD 94.0 billion") stays `revenue`.
- **b.** A table label, or a prose word, that is a ledger subject's name (a word in `Evidence.names`, matched case-sensitively as the name path matches it) is not read as a field cue or field label. "price target", "target price" and "target of" keep the `target` cue: they are not bare names.
- Not changed: the evidence side, the declared typing, `_resolve_subject`, every other cue, `shared/`, the registry.
