## Driver

Phase 1 (TP-023) changed what happens to an unverified number; it did not
change which numbers verify. On main 97b4a88 the ADR-008 probe still
shows both failure directions (ADR-008 Amendment 1, Context):

- four correct renderings are flagged (scale word, decline sign,
  whole-number percent, ISO time);
- four misattributions pass unflagged (a news count as a P/E, another
  symbol's close, a sentiment score as a net margin, title digits as
  revenue), and so does the DE-12 comparison-table swap.

Open debt this plan repays: DE-12 (symbol-level typing), DE-10 (a), (b)
and (c), DE-13 (partial-date residuals), and the ADR-006 Am7 phase-2
limitations (non-OHLCV field swaps, 1-decimal percents, number words).

## Behavior under test

1. **Every digit is classified.** Each digit run of an answer belongs to
   exactly one claim (structural, identifier, quoted, temporal,
   quantitative) or one exempt span. Layout rules (BD-016, Am6) and the
   Am8 identifier set are unchanged; symbol-shaped tickers are
   identifiers (P4); Q1-Q4, H1/H2 and FY labels stay identifiers (P7).
2. **Typed evidence.** Evidence values are indexed by subject, field
   class, unit and currency (Appendix A). Free text (titles, notes, URLs)
   serves only quoted claims (P3). List lengths are count evidence (P4).
   Fields a tool reported unavailable are known per subject.
3. **Quantitative claims with a field** match only evidence of their
   subject and a compatible field class (`unknown` keys are compatible),
   at the written precision: half a unit of the last written digit,
   whole numbers included; percent (marker required), basis points,
   multiples, scale words, decline sign and currency handled.
4. **Quantitative claims without a field** keep the v1 rule against every
   non-free-text value (P2): never looser than v1.
5. **Subjects** come from table headers, then the nearest mention in the
   sentence (preceding, then following), then the paragraph; none matches
   any subject. "respectively" pairs in order.
6. **Temporal claims** are parsed whole and compared in UTC at the
   written precision. More precise than the evidence is unverified. A
   month, quarter or year overlapping an evidence window verifies (P1). A
   day must equal an evidence date. The DE-13 year rules apply (sentence
   subject, a table label that is a time word, a range with a time cue).
7. **Quoted claims** verify only when the quoted span appears in tool
   text.
8. **Number words** with a percent, scale, currency or unit word are
   quantitative claims (P5).
9. **Marking by claim position.** Only unverified claims carry `[?]`,
   after the word that ends the claim; a verified occurrence of the same
   digits stays unmarked. Removing the markers returns the text exactly.
   A claim position that cannot be located still withholds (fail-closed).
10. **One reason per unverified claim** (ADR-008 Am1 decision 9). The
    notice groups claims by reason; the phase-1 wording stays for
    not-found claims. The record adds `unverified_detail`; `/show` lists
    claims with reasons.
11. **Phase-1 behavior is unchanged:** flag instead of withhold, the
    history note, the streak notice, flagged reports, INV-002 and the
    language withhold.
12. **Measured.** E-010: recall 1.00 on both corpora; precision 1.00 on
    the development set and at least 0.90 on the held-out set, every
    false flag pinned as a named residual.

## Boundary cases (R8)

- Rounding: exactly half a unit of the last written digit (inclusive)
  and the next representable value beyond it, for 0-4 decimals and each
  scale word.
- Windows: a month that ends the day before a window opens; a month
  containing the window's first day.
- Subjects: a subject mention immediately before versus after the number;
  a paragraph break between mention and claim.
- Trailing zeros: `19,000,000` versus `19 million`.

## Amendment 1 (2026-10-02) — held-out measurement, refinements, two owner decisions

**First held-out run.** The engine was frozen (source hash `6ce7b767`)
before the held-out corpus was evaluated. Result:

- precision 156/170 = 0.918;
- recall 68/80 = 0.850;
- no flags on unlabeled numbers.

Recall missed the 1.00 target. The refinements below were made in
response. Each is pinned by its own development item (D033-D048),
written independently of the held-out texts.

1. **Sentiment components.** They were one class. They are now the
   sentiment score (polarity) plus positive, negative and neutral shares,
   with cues such as "positive component" and "neutral share".
2. **API calls.** They were one class. They are now four: used,
   remaining, cap and last run.
   - A generic count is narrowed by nearby words:
     - "remain", "left", "leaves": remaining;
     - "of N" or "out of N": cap;
     - "last run": the last run;
     - "used", "consumed": used.
   - New cues "API budget" and "call budget".
3. **Days and times by subject.** They are checked against the claim's
   subject first. A date that belongs to another symbol is unverified,
   with the reason "this date belongs to <symbol>".
4. **False-flag fixes.**
   - The news `stored` count is typed as a news count.
   - Clauses no longer split at ":".
   - Close cues: "settled at", "finished at", "ended at".
   - Drawdown cues: "deepest", "largest", "worst" (and similar) with
     "decline", "drop", "fall", "loss".
   - "% of revenue" (and "percent of revenue") is a margin cue, net or
     gross.
   - New cue "tokens".
   - A cue after a number does not reach across a conjunction or another
     number.
   - A number introduced by a comparison ("down from", "against",
     "versus", "than") inherits the field of the preceding claim.
   - After "from", a number is a level, not a change.

**Second run.**

- precision 168/170 = 0.988;
- recall 75/80 = 0.938;
- no flags on unlabeled numbers.

For the refined classes these numbers are not an independent
measurement.
