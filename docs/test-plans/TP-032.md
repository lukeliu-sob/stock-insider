## Driver

TP-030's P6 note: once the first pattern fix is tuned against battery 1, battery 1 becomes development data. A post-fix recall claim then needs a second fresh battery, written by a separate author. This TP writes that battery and seals it before any pattern change (TP-033).

## Battery provenance (P6)

- Author: a separately briefed subagent with a fresh context. Its brief named the three gap classes, the three control classes, and the INV-002 wording (the one entry of `docs/architecture/invariants.md`). It was told not to open any other repository file, in particular nothing under `src/` or `test/` and no existing battery. Its examples were invented for illustration.
- The orchestrating session wrote the brief from the same class definitions as TP-030. Before writing it, the orchestrating session had seen battery 1's measured results (TP-030). The sealed battery's author had not seen battery 1.
- The sealed battery was checked for overlap with battery 1: no exact duplicate sentences and no near duplicates (word-set Jaccard below 0.6).

## The battery

`test/offline/fixtures/inv002_sealed_tp032.json`: 60 items, ids SB-001 to SB-060, with the same class structure as TP-030.

- 30 `flag` items (INV-002 violations), 10 each in `mis-scoped-hedge`, `fabricated-attribution`, `unanticipated-paraphrase`.
- 30 `pass` items (compliant), 10 each in `control-hypothesis`, `control-scoped-hedge`, `control-factual`.
- Each item has id, sentence, expected, gap_class and rationale. ASCII only, one sentence per item.
- SHA-256 of the LF-normalized file: `b2625419a7ca908fb110dfbf2279cb4877af0079014ea98d983d33dc675ab243`.

## Sealing

- Committed in this TP's PR, before any pattern change.
- Not used for tuning. It is measured exactly twice: once before the fix (the baseline above, counts only), and once after the final fix, as the result. The measurement prints counts, never sentences. Battery 1 is used for reporting only.
- The seal is checked by a test that compares the file's SHA-256 with the value recorded above, so any change to the labels or sentences is detected.

## Owner decisions carried into the fix (2026-10-04)

- Hedge rule: ADR-006 Am8 stands (a clause-local modal hedge is a label), so the `control-scoped-hedge` labels are unchanged.
- Attribution rule: generic attribution tags ("the consensus view", "our proprietary signal") are not institutional sources, so they are labeled `flag`.
- Recall target for the fix: at least 80% recall on this battery's flag set, and zero false flags on its controls.
