## Driver

Live session 2026-10-01 (deepseek-flash, profile standard). The turn
"give me info about BYD" ran six tools successfully (watchlist.list,
symbol.search, market.quote, market.indicators, fundamentals.summary,
news.recent) and the user saw only:

```
data unavailable for: 2025
(tools: 6 · post-check: failed · tokens: 13377)
```

Root cause (reproduced offline; the quarantined original sits in
`sessions/`, outside the agent access map, and was not read):

1. `market.indicators` computes volatility and max drawdown over the
   last 260 stored bars, so for a symbol with a year of history the
   window start (`risk.volatility.window.from`) and often the drawdown
   `peak_date` are dates in 2025.
2. The model rendered such a date at month or year precision
   ("since September 2025", "late 2025").
3. The post-check folds only full dates (ISO, BD-018/ADR-006 Am4;
   natural, TP-019/Am8) into the pool's compact `YYYYMMDD` form. The
   evidence carries `2025-09-25` only as `20250925`, never as a bare
   `2025`, so the orphan `2025` matched nothing and the whole answer was
   quarantined.

| The model writes (evidence date 2025-09-25) | Before TP-020 |
|---|---|
| `2025-09-25` | pass |
| `September 25, 2025` | pass |
| `September 2025`, `Oct 2025` | fail: 2025 |
| `late 2025` | fail: 2025 |
| `in 2024` (no 2024 date in evidence) | fail: 2024 (correct) |

The class was already recorded as a residual ("prose years",
evaluation logbook E-005/E-006, `test_faithfulness_eval.py`) parked for
an owner extraction-semantics decision. That decision is now taken:
bare years are allowed.

## Owner decision and its safe form

Allow month-years and bare years that the evidence supports. A bare
four-digit number is ambiguous: "late 2025" is a time, "closed at 2026"
is a price. Pool membership alone would let any fabricated value that
equals an evidence year pass (the BD-020 class the fifth audit closed).
The allowance therefore applies only to a year written as a time
reference; everything else stays a numeric claim (fail-closed).

## Semantics

Applied in `postcheck_numbers` after the existing full-date folds, on
the response text only:

1. Evidence calendar: every date-shaped string in the session ledger
   (ISO dates and datetimes, compact seendate stamps, natural full
   dates, month-years, ISO year-months) contributes its year `YYYY` and
   month `YYYYMM`. Numbers never contribute: a volume of 202509 makes no
   month citable.
2. Month-year references: `September 2025`, `Sep. 2025`,
   `September, 2025`, ISO `2025-09` -> key `YYYYMM`.
3. Bare-year references: a 4-digit 19xx/20xx not glued to letters,
   digits, currency symbols or a decimal/thousands separator, and not a
   ticker code (`2020.HK`), with a temporal frame:
   - a cue right before it: in, since, during, throughout, until, till,
     before, after, early, mid(-), late, end/start/beginning/middle/rest/
     half of, as of, the year, year-end, fiscal, calendar, full-year, FY,
     Q1-Q4, H1/H2, spring/summer/autumn/winter;
   - or a cue right after it: 's, Q1-Q4, H1/H2, peak(s), trough(s),
     high(s), low(s), level(s), window, period, results, report, annual,
     interim, earnings, guidance, year-end, full-year;
   - or a range link (-, en/em dash, /, &, to, through, thru, and) to a
     framed year ("late 2025 to 2026", "the 2025-2026 window").
   A value frame right after the year (%, percent, pct, bp/bps, x,
   times, k, mn, bn, thousand/million/billion/trillion, shares, units,
   points/pts, dollars, yuan, HKD/USD/RMB/CNY/CNH/EUR/GBP/JPY) cancels
   the frame for the year and for every year linked to it.
4. A reference whose key is in the evidence calendar is verified and
   leaves the numeric check; it is listed in `NumberCheck.calendar` for
   audit. A reference the evidence does not support stays in the text
   and fails in the numeric check exactly as before (the degraded line
   is unchanged: `data unavailable for: 2024`).
5. Full dates keep day precision: "26 September 2025" against evidence
   2025-09-25 still fails as `20250926`, even though September 2025 is
   an evidence month.

`test_full_dates_keep_day_precision`, the "from 2025 to 2026" case and

## Boundary cases (R8)

Evidence month vs the neighbouring month (September vs August 2025);
evidence year vs ±1 (2025 passes, 2024 and 2027 fail); day precision
(25 vs 26 September 2025); framed vs unframed year ("in 2025" vs
"2025 was"); framed year 2026 vs the ticker-shaped "in 2026.HK".
